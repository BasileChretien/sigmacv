# research

The consent-gated research-logging pipeline (this project is also a research vehicle). The study is Nagoya University's observational study **2026-0310**, approved by the Bioethics Review Committee of the Graduate School of Medicine on 2026-10-21 (PI Branko Aleksic, co-investigator Basile Chrétien). The approved documents (protocol 第1.2版, 説明書/同意書 第1.1版, EN Information Sheet/Consent Form Version 1.0, 別添1 the consent screen) live in the maintainer's `Ethics/` folder, outside the repository.

- **`enabled.ts`** — the **master switch** `isResearchLoggingEnabled()` (env `RESEARCH_LOGGING_ENABLED`, **default OFF**). While off, `logCvSave` writes nothing (checked before the consent read), the editor link and the consent form do not exist, the consent route refuses a grant, and `/cv/research-sheet` is not found for anyone without a record. **Withdrawal never depends on it** (GDPR Art. 7(3)): an account holding a consent (current or earlier version) keeps the account-menu entry, its status and "Stop taking part", and the route always accepts a withdrawal. It goes on in production once the research panel is deployed.
- **`log.ts`** — writes `ResearchEvent` rows. **It writes nothing unless the master switch is on AND `User.researchConsent` is true at the current `RESEARCH_CONSENT_VERSION`.** This gate is mandatory (GDPR + Japan APPI; logging only under the approved protocol, confirmatory analyses pre-registered). It re-exports the version from `consent/`.
- **`consent/`** — the participant-facing consent of study 2026-0310, client-safe (the editor's panel and the API share it). `index.ts` holds `RESEARCH_CONSENT_VERSION` (**2** = the approved study; 1 was the notice before approval), `APPROVED_CONSENT_LOCALES` (**en-US and ja-JP only**: the protocol admits only people who understand the consent language, and only those two are approved), `CONSENT_DOCUMENT_VERSIONS` (consent version × language → the approved document a record names), `CONSENT_DOCUMENT_LABELS` (the same document as the screen names it; the version integer is never shown, only kept in the record and the downloads) and the fallback: any other UI locale shows the English text, with a JA/EN switch. `en-US.ts` / `ja-JP.ts` are the screen text (title, five summary points, checkboxes ① and ②, buttons, contacts); `sheet.<locale>.ts` is the full information sheet, **generated** word for word from the approved .docx by `scripts/consent-sheet-from-docx.py` (never hand-edit; regenerate, then `prettier --write`). Adding one of the eight other languages after an amendment = its two files, its `CONSENT_DOCUMENT_VERSIONS` and `CONSENT_DOCUMENT_LABELS` entries and the allowlist; nothing else. `status.ts` derives the panel's state and the downloadable record, `request.ts` validates the POST body, `sheetPage.ts` builds the printable `/cv/research-sheet`. **Never call the research data anonymous in any language**: the approved sheet says it is pseudonymised, NOT anonymised (`tests/research-consent-text.test.ts` enforces it).
- **The electronic record** (`ResearchConsentRecord`, 別添1 §4–5) — `POST /api/account/consent` refuses a grant without `agreeRequired: true` (checkbox ①: the consent is the tick), then writes a `grant` row (version, language shown, ① and ②) with the `User` fields in one transaction; unless the account already held the current consent, that same transaction deletes its `ResearchEvent` rows (events logged under an older version's notice, or stray ones, never travel on under the approved consent). A withdrawal deletes the `ResearchEvent` rows, clears the `User` fields and writes a `withdraw` row in one transaction; with no consent to withdraw it records nothing (a no-op 200, the events still swept). Rows are never edited and survive a withdrawal; they go with the account. `GET /api/account/consent/record` downloads them; the account export includes them.
- **`diff.ts`** — computes the curation deltas that are the actual research signal (e.g. "mine/not-mine" corrections, composition choices), so logged events are minimal and structured rather than raw snapshots.
- **`export.ts`** — the **IRB-gated researcher export** (pure core). `researchExportGate()` is **OFF unless** the env triad `RESEARCH_EXPORT_ENABLED=true` + `RESEARCH_EXPORT_IRB_REF` + `RESEARCH_EXPORT_PSEUDONYM_SALT` (≥16 chars) is set. `buildResearchExport()` de-identifies: subjects become keyed-HMAC **pseudonyms** (never the user id), only **pre-registered** event types are emitted, and payloads are the already-minimized `diff.ts` signals (no names/emails/ORCIDs). `buildKeyTable()` produces the **separate re-identification key table (対照表)** — the `subject` pseudonym mapped back to `userId`/name/email/ORCID — which the **個人情報管理者 (personal-information manager)** holds under access control to honour withdrawal/erasure; it is **never** given to the analyst, who works from the de-identified dataset alone (protocol §4.2.1). The actual DB read + JSONL write is the **offline** operator script `scripts/export-research-data.ts` (`npm run research:export`), which writes the two files separately (the key table as `KEYTABLE-DO-NOT-SHARE_*`) — there is **deliberately no network route**. Keep `PRE_REGISTERED_EVENT_TYPES` in sync with the `type` strings written in `log.ts`. **Do not enable** until the protocol is approved and fields are finalised to the pre-registration.

Treat the flag and the consent check as hard preconditions — never log on a path that could run without them, and keep events data-minimized. The export inherits the same posture: gated off, pseudonymous, minimal.
